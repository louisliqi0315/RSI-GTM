# Stage Summary (English) — Stages 2a–2k

**Purpose:** One-page English summary of the stage designs and their conclusion limits, for readers of the arXiv preprint *Towards RSI-GTM: Trust-Constrained, Verification-Gated Recursive Self-Improvement for Go-to-Market* (Qi Li, Independent Researcher).

**Status of this summary:** It is a translation of the stage table in the original frozen package's README (which itself summarised the stage reports). **The stage reports in `reports-zh/` (Chinese originals) govern in case of any discrepancy.** No new number is introduced here; every number below is taken from the original reports or the original README table. Rows for different designs must not be averaged, mixed, or used to overwrite one another.

**Primary metric throughout:** Pipeline-per-Trust. Paired differences are against the static policy unless stated otherwise. **All results are synthetic** (simulator only); no result here is a real vendor effect.

Arm F **+0.0089, F−D0 +0.0000**, only 1 fallback adoption in 150 slots (seed 28), all other vetoes all_candidates_failed — under the binary candidate set, fallback has almost no increment; but **the test is unfair**: F's candidate set contains no W2, so seeds 6 and 29 in F were already rank-0 W1 adoptions and fallback was never triggered; F's "not shown effective" may only be read as "almost no increment under the binary-candidate-set + independent-validation-seed design", **never as "the fallback rule is generally ineffective"**; the fallback branch is not closed. All three arms: total progressive adoptions 0. |

## Unified workflow-layer wording (paper)

> A one-off small gain under the 24-week design, still a marginal effect of the same order at 48 weeks with the interval at the zero line's knife edge, with horizon and epoch not significantly changing it, and not shown to scale with time or rounds.

Independent validation seeds are stated only as breaking depth 1 in the adoption-count sense, with progression 0, increment unproven, and the effect almost disappearing under dual-sample confirmation. Fallback is stated only as almost no increment under the binary candidate set plus independent-validation-seed design (unfair test).

## What still cannot be claimed

- Gated recursion improved efficiency or conversion at real vendors or on real logs.
- Fitted Q evaluation, or any offline evaluation method, cannot be claimed to have validated a real strategy.
- Public signals predict developer ramp rates, or media-reported figures in the China map are financial facts.
- Joint recursion better than static; trust budget as a general primitive; adoption rule as a general principle or no-false-improvement guarantee; evaluator separation as necessary; workflow recursion gain proven or strengthening with rounds.
